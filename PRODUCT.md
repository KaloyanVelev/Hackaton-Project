# Street Aware

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js, TypeScript, PostgreSQL with PostGIS, and MapLibre. The first handoff runs locally in Docker Compose with online basemaps.

## Users

People in Bulgaria who want to explore where crashes were recorded and see details for a specific crash.

## Product Purpose

Make it easy to move from a national view of recorded crash concentrations to the source location and details of a crash.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The map has Hotspots and Crashes views. Visitors can switch between Streets and Satellite without losing their place. The interface opens in Bulgarian and also supports English. Future date and severity filters are outside the first demo.

## Brand Commitments

Use the supplied Street Aware wordmarks. Streets uses charcoal and grayscale. Satellite uses white around the imagery. Crash colors follow the supplied severity scheme.

## Evidence on Hand

The supplied crash snapshot contains 102,103 rows from January 2024 through November 2026. The map identifies the reported coverage and counts rows that lack a usable in-country location.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard access to the controls and a list of mapped crashes. Use marker shape as well as color to convey severity.
